About

A builder shaped by curiosity, mathematics, and community.

I use software and AI as practical tools: to understand complex systems, expand access, and build things that are dependable in the real world.

01ORIGIN

My path into technology started in Benin with a summer school on artificial intelligence. It was the moment mathematics stopped being only abstract and became a way to solve tangible problems.

That opened the way to Software Engineering at the University of Abomey-Calavi, where I combined rigorous technical study with a commitment to social impact. Competitions, research, and product work taught me to value the people and constraints around a system as much as the model or architecture inside it.

Now, as an incoming master’s student at Carnegie Mellon University Africa, I am focused on engineering AI systems that are useful in resource-constrained environments across Africa.

“Curiosity is the start. Usefulness is the test.”

— My working principle
